## 1. Scope

This policy is intended to apply to cosmetic listings and digital content offered through an Alpha-operated Store, once the seller, purchase flow, delivery, and consumer terms have been confirmed.

A catalogue entry, preview, price label, or planned item is not by itself an offer that can be purchased.

## 2. Current verified position

The Launcher source inspected for the policy work contains a cosmetic catalogue and Store interface. The inspected Supabase application schema did not contain payment-card or payment-transaction tables. These observations do not rule out an external provider or a separate payment flow; none has been verified.

## 3. Listings and descriptions

Before a digital item is offered for sale, its listing must clearly state:
- the item's name and material characteristics;
- the price and currency, including mandatory charges;
- any technical requirements or compatibility limits;
- whether the item is permanent, time-limited, consumable, or conditional;
- how and when it is delivered or made available;
- any restrictions on use, transfer, resale, or account eligibility;
- the identity of the seller and a usable contact route.

Do not describe a cosmetic as owned, delivered, or guaranteed merely because it appears in a catalogue or preview.

## 4. Orders and payment

No purchase process is defined by this draft.

Before enabling checkout, Alpha must document the seller, order confirmation, total price, payment provider, payment data received by Alpha, payment authorization, failed payments, duplicate payments, receipts, order records, and support process.

**Payment and order procedure:** [REQUIRES ALPHA CONFIRMATION AND LEGAL REVIEW]

Alpha should not collect or store full payment-card credentials unless a separately reviewed and lawful payment architecture explicitly requires it. Any provider handling payment information must be identified in the Privacy Policy.

## 6. Withdrawal, refunds, and remedies

Mandatory Dutch and EU consumer rights may apply to digital content and digital services. The availability and timing of any withdrawal right, and any exception for immediate digital delivery, depend on the actual contract flow and the legally required information and express consent/acknowledgement.

This draft does not waive statutory rights and does not promise a blanket no-refund rule.

Before sales begin, Alpha must provide legally reviewed information about:
- the right of withdrawal and any applicable exception;
- required express consent and acknowledgement for immediate digital supply, where relevant;
- refunds for failed, defective, misdescribed, or non-conforming digital content;
- statutory conformity remedies and support;
- complaint handling and response route.

**Final withdrawal/refund/remedy terms:** [REQUIRES ALPHA CONFIRMATION AND LEGAL REVIEW]

## 7. Promotions, discounts, and bundles

Any promotion must state its conditions, duration, eligibility, and relevant price information clearly. Alpha must not use misleading reference prices, scarcity claims, or undisclosed restrictions.

**Promotion approval and pricing rules:** [REQUIRES ALPHA CONFIRMATION]

## 8. Licences and permitted use

A digital item should be used only with the Alpha account and within the scope stated in its listing and applicable licence. Ownership of a digital entitlement does not transfer copyright or other intellectual-property rights in the underlying artwork, software, or design.

**Item licence and transferability:** [REQUIRES ALPHA CONFIRMATION]

## 9. Account restrictions and purchased content

Before sales begin, Alpha must explain what happens to paid entitlements if an account is suspended, deleted, compromised, or inaccessible, subject to mandatory consumer law. A moderation action must not be used to remove statutory consumer remedies.

**Purchased-content treatment after account action:** [REQUIRES ALPHA CONFIRMATION AND LEGAL REVIEW]

## 10. Third-party platforms

If a third party processes payments, distributes content, or hosts a Store component, Alpha must identify that party's role and explain which terms apply. Do not imply that a third party endorses Alpha unless that relationship is verified.

## 11. Changes and discontinuation

Before offering content, Alpha must define how it will notify users about material changes, item removal, service discontinuation, and any effect on existing entitlements. Mandatory consumer rights remain unaffected.
