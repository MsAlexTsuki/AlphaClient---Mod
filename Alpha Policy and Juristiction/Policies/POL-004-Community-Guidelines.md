> **Draft notice:** These guidelines are a working draft for Alpha-operated community spaces and features. They do not replace the rules of independent Minecraft servers or third-party platforms.

## 1. Purpose and scope

These guidelines describe the expected standard of conduct when using Alpha community features, including profiles, friend requests, private messages, presence features, and any Alpha-operated community space that is formally brought within scope.

They apply to users regardless of rank. A rank, badge, staff role, or account status does not exempt anyone from these guidelines.

## 2. Treat people respectfully

You must not:
- harass, threaten, bully, or repeatedly target another person;
- use discriminatory slurs or attack people based on protected or personal characteristics;
- encourage violence or credible threats;
- coordinate harassment or encourage others to target a user;
- continue unwanted contact after a clear request to stop, subject to legitimate support or moderation communication.

Disagreement, criticism, and good-faith debate are allowed when expressed without abuse or targeted harassment.

## 3. Profiles and user-generated content

When using a profile, avatar, biography, or other user-submitted content:
- use content you have the right to use;
- do not impersonate another person or an official Alpha account;
- do not publish private information about another person without authorization;
- do not use content that is threatening, harassing, unlawful, or designed to deceive;
- remember that profile information and avatar visibility may be broader than a private conversation.

The current Supabase configuration inspected for POL-002 makes profile avatars publicly readable. Do not upload an image that you do not want to be publicly accessible.

## 4. Friends and private messages

Use friend requests and private messages for genuine communication. Do not use them for spam, scams, phishing, threats, harassment, or unsolicited mass promotion.

Do not assume that a private message is an end-to-end encrypted communication. Do not share passwords, authentication codes, payment credentials, or sensitive personal information in messages.

The current database policy restricts private-message reads to the sender and recipient and restricts sending to friends. This describes the inspected database policy, not a guarantee against device compromise, account compromise, legal disclosure, or every possible operational access path. See POL-002.

## 5. Names, ranks, and representation

Do not falsely claim to be Founder, Owner, staff, a partner, a verified person, or an official representative. Do not use another person's badge, identity, or account to mislead users.

Rank names and permissions are governed by POL-006. Community status does not authorize staff actions.

## 6. Advertising and promotion

Do not flood community features with repetitive promotions, referral links, unsolicited commercial messages, or misleading offers. Any official partnership or promotion must be clearly authorized by Alpha.

## 7. Reporting and moderation

If you experience or observe a violation, use the official reporting route. Include relevant context and avoid publicly reposting private conversations or personal information.

Alpha may review reports and take proportionate action under POL-003 and POL-005. No particular moderation workflow, response time, or appeal outcome is promised until confirmed.

## 8. No retaliation

Do not threaten, harass, expose, or punish someone for making a good-faith report, participating in a review, or raising a concern. Knowingly false reports or deliberate evidence manipulation may themselves be reviewed.

Each published revision will show its version and effective date. Material changes will be communicated as required by applicable law.
