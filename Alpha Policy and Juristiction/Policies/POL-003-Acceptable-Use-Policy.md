## 1. Purpose

This policy sets baseline rules for safe, lawful, and fair use of Alpha services. It applies alongside POL-001 (Terms of Service), POL-004 (Community Guidelines), and any feature-specific rules shown in Alpha.

## 2. Scope

This policy is intended to cover Alpha Launcher, Alpha Client, Alpha Accounts, and Alpha-operated community features made available to the user. It applies when using an Alpha account, connecting to Alpha services, communicating through Alpha features, or interacting with Alpha staff.

A rule in this policy applies only to a feature that Alpha actually operates or makes available. It does not make Alpha responsible for independent Minecraft servers, third-party platforms, or services outside Alpha's control.

## 3. Account and access security

You must:
- use only accounts you own or are explicitly authorized to use;
- keep your sign-in methods and recovery methods reasonably secure;
- provide accurate information where Alpha requires it;
- report suspected account compromise or unauthorized access;
- use ranks and permissions only within the access granted to your account.

You must not:
- impersonate another user, staff member, or Alpha representative;
- share, sell, rent, or transfer account access where this creates unauthorized access or violates applicable account rules;
- bypass authentication, verification, rank checks, rate limits, or other access controls;
- access another user's private messages, profile controls, saves, or staff information without authorization;
- use automation or scripts to probe or overload Alpha services without written permission.

## 4. Prohibited conduct

You must not use Alpha services to:
- threaten, harass, stalk, intimidate, or target another person;
- publish another person's private or identifying information without a lawful basis and appropriate authorization;
- send spam, scams, phishing messages, malicious links, or deceptive promotions;
- distribute malware or attempt to compromise a user's device, account, or Alpha infrastructure;
- exploit bugs to obtain unauthorized access, items, ranks, permissions, data, or other advantages;
- interfere with service availability, integrity, or other users' ability to use Alpha;
- violate applicable law or infringe intellectual-property or privacy rights;
- evade a valid restriction by creating or using another account.

## 5. Software, game clients, and third-party environments

Use Alpha Client and Launcher in accordance with their displayed functionality, license terms, and the rules of the server or platform on which you choose to use them.

Alpha does not set the rules of independent Minecraft servers. A feature being present in Alpha Client does not mean that a third-party server permits it. You are responsible for checking and following the rules of the environment in which you play.

Do not misrepresent Alpha's relationship with Mojang, Microsoft, a server operator, or another third party.

## 6. Security research and vulnerability testing

Do not test, scan, exploit, or attempt to gain access to systems you do not own or have permission to test. Good-faith reports about suspected vulnerabilities should be sent through the official reporting route once Alpha publishes one.

## 7. Enforcement

Depending on the facts, severity, risk, and applicable law, Alpha may review a report and consider measures such as:
- a warning or request to stop the conduct;
- removal or restriction of offending content where Alpha controls it;
- temporary restriction of a relevant feature or permission;
- temporary account or service restriction;
- permanent restriction or account termination in serious or repeated cases.

These are possible measures, not a claim that every measure is currently implemented. The actual decision-maker, evidence process, notice, duration, and appeal route must be confirmed and published before this section is used as an enforcement promise.

Provide the relevant account name, feature, approximate date/time, and a concise description. Include only evidence that you are entitled to share. Do not post private messages, personal data, or security-sensitive details publicly.
