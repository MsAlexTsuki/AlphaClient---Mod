# AI Usage and Transparency Policy

> **Draft notice:** This document describes the use of AI assistance during Alpha development. It is a transparency statement, not a claim that every component was generated by AI or that AI-generated output is automatically correct.

## 1. Purpose and scope

Alpha acknowledges that artificial intelligence (AI) tools have been used to assist with the development of Alpha Launcher and related Alpha project materials.

This policy explains the areas in which AI assistance has been used and clarifies the distinction between AI-assisted work and decisions made by the project owner.

It applies to AI assistance used for Alpha Launcher, Alpha Client project work, the Alpha Store, policies, documentation, ideas, bug investigation, and changelog preparation.

## 2. Launcher development

AI assistance has been used during the creation and development of Alpha Launcher and related launcher content.

This assistance has included helping with development tasks, exploring technical approaches, preparing and refining implementation ideas, and assisting with programming-related work.

This does not mean that the entire Launcher was generated automatically by AI. The project owner provides the direction and requirements and decides which proposed work is accepted.

## 3. Idea generation and feature planning

AI has been used as a brainstorming assistant to generate and develop ideas for Alpha.

Examples include:
- Suggesting ideas for new features, modules, and improvements.
- Exploring possible functionality and user experiences.
- Developing initial concepts into more detailed feature proposals.
- Helping organize and structure ideas for future development.

AI-generated ideas are suggestions only. The project owner decides which ideas are accepted, changed, postponed, or rejected.

## 4. Bug investigation and technical explanations

AI has been used to help investigate bugs and technical problems that the project owner found difficult to understand independently.

This assistance has included:
- Explaining unfamiliar code and programming concepts.
- Reviewing error messages and describing possible causes.
- Helping trace unexpected behavior through relevant code.
- Suggesting possible fixes and explaining their intended effects.

AI explanations and proposed fixes are not guaranteed to be correct. They must be checked against the actual source code, project requirements, and observed behavior. A suggested fix must not be described as successful unless it has been verified.

## 5. Alpha Store development

AI assistance was used to create and develop the Alpha Store website because the project owner experienced difficulty implementing the Store independently.

AI assisted with constructing and organizing website components and presentation. The project owner supplied the intended direction and requirements and determines which results belong in Alpha.

This acknowledgement does not imply that every Store component or item of content was generated by AI.

## 6. Policy creation and documentation

AI has been used to help create and organize Alpha policy documents and other project documentation.

This assistance has included drafting policy text, structuring documents, improving clarity, organizing related subjects, and preparing longer documentation.

AI-assisted policy drafts are not automatically approved, legally reviewed, legally binding, or effective. Their approval and publication status must be stated separately.

## 7. Changelog preparation

AI has been used to prepare detailed and extended changelogs for Alpha releases.

This includes translating technical changes into structured descriptions that Alpha users can understand, and organizing related changes into readable release notes.

Changelogs must reflect changes that were actually made. AI must not invent features, omit material changes intentionally, or claim that functionality was tested or works when that has not been verified.

## 8. Human responsibility and review

The project owner retains responsibility for Alpha's direction, requirements, priorities, and decisions about which AI-assisted suggestions or outputs are accepted.

AI tools act as assistants. They do not independently approve releases, determine access permissions, set project policy, or make final product decisions on behalf of Alpha.

AI-generated code, explanations, ideas, policy text, and documentation may require review, correction, modification, or rejection before use.

## 9. Transparency and limits of this statement

Alpha does not represent all of its development, website content, documentation, or release materials as having been created entirely without AI assistance.

This policy acknowledges the uses described above. It does not establish that every file, feature, line of code, design element, or release note was produced by AI.

The exact contribution of AI may differ between individual tasks and project components.

## 10. Updates

This policy may be updated if Alpha's use of AI tools changes or if additional forms of AI assistance become relevant.

Each published version must display its version and effective date. Until formally approved and published, this document remains a draft.
