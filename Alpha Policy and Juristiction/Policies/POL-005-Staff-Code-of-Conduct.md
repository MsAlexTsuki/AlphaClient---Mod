> **Draft notice:** This is an internal staff standard. It is not a grant of permissions and does not replace technical access controls, employment/volunteer agreements, or applicable law.

## 1. Purpose

Staff access exists to operate, support, moderate, and protect Alpha. Anyone given staff permissions is expected to act honestly, consistently, proportionately, and only for legitimate Alpha purposes.

## 2. Who it applies to

This draft applies to people assigned an Alpha staff rank or granted staff-specific permissions, including Trial Moderator (T-Mod), Moderator, Sr Moderator, Admin, Sr Admin, Platform Admin, Manager, Developer, Owner, and Founder.

A rank title alone does not establish that a person currently has every permission associated with that title. Actual access is determined by the configured permission system and the rules in POL-006.

## 3. Core duties

Staff must:
- act in Alpha's legitimate interests and avoid using access for personal advantage;
- treat users consistently and respectfully;
- use the minimum access needed for the task;
- keep private user information and internal records confidential;
- record staff actions accurately where the relevant system provides an audit or activity record;
- explain decisions clearly when disclosure is appropriate;
- report security incidents, permission errors, conflicts of interest, and suspected misuse;
- follow POL-002 when handling personal data.

## 4. Prohibited staff conduct

Staff must not:
- access private messages, player notes, account data, or other records out of curiosity or for personal reasons;
- disclose internal notes, private messages, audit details, or personal data to unauthorized people;
- use staff permissions to threaten, harass, retaliate against, or unfairly target a user;
- alter ranks, permissions, verification, or records without authority and a legitimate reason;
- use commands, tools, or staff modules outside their assigned scope;
- share staff credentials or allow another person to use their staff session;
- falsify, conceal, or deliberately omit relevant staff records;
- use staff status to obtain gifts, money, services, or other personal benefits from users;
- interfere with a review or retaliate against a reporter or witness.

## 5. Access boundaries and rank-specific restrictions

Staff must follow the access boundaries defined by Alpha's rank and permission configuration.

The following project rules are mandatory when implementing and administering access:
- Founder is exclusive to the project owner and is not an ordinary assignable rank.
- Owner does not receive access to the private Founder folder.
- Founder has the Founder-level access intended by the project owner.
- Manager and Platform Admin access must remain within their respective configured scopes.
- Bhop belongs only under StaffMods → General and must be permission-gated.
- StaffMods is not public; access is limited to eligible staff permissions.
- Multiple assigned ranks may contribute permissions, while only the highest applicable rank is displayed where the product uses a single displayed rank.

These statements define required boundaries. They do not certify that every current permission check is defect-free. Any mismatch must be reported and corrected through an authorized development change, not bypassed by staff.

## 6. Confidentiality and personal data

Treat private messages, staff notes, player notes, account identifiers, moderation evidence, and audit records as restricted information. Share them only with people who need them for an authorized task and only through an approved channel.

Do not copy personal data into external documents, screenshots, chats, or third-party services unless there is a documented need and authorization.

**Approved staff communication and evidence-storage channels:** [REQUIRES ALPHA CONFIRMATION]

## 7. Conflicts of interest

Disclose a personal relationship, dispute, financial interest, or other conflict that could affect an impartial decision. Where practical, ask another authorized staff member to handle the matter.

## 8. Moderation decisions

Staff must apply published rules consistently, consider relevant context, avoid disproportionate measures, and keep records where the system supports them. Staff must not promise a particular sanction or outcome before the review is complete.

**Decision authority, escalation levels, and appeal ownership:** [REQUIRES ALPHA CONFIRMATION]

## 9. Security incidents and mistakes

Report suspected account compromise, data exposure, permission bypass, lost staff device, or accidental disclosure promptly through the designated internal route. Do not conceal an incident or independently delete evidence.

**Internal incident contact and escalation route:** [REQUIRES ALPHA CONFIRMATION]

## 10. Breaches of this Code

A suspected breach may be reviewed by an authorized person who is not improperly involved in the matter. Possible outcomes may include guidance, removal of a specific permission, temporary suspension of staff access, or removal from a staff role, subject to the actual authority structure and applicable law.
