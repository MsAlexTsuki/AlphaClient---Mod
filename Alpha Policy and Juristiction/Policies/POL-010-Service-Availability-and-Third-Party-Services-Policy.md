> **Draft notice:** This policy describes how availability and external dependencies should be communicated. It does not promise a service level, uptime percentage, support schedule, or uninterrupted operation.

## 1. Scope

This policy is intended to cover Alpha-operated software and online features, including Alpha Launcher, Alpha Client services, Alpha Accounts, community features, and related backend functions.

It does not make Alpha responsible for independent Minecraft servers, Microsoft services, Mojang services, internet providers, or other third-party systems.

## 2. Availability

Alpha services may be affected by maintenance, software updates, outages, security incidents, network failures, infrastructure problems, or third-party interruptions.

Unless Alpha publishes a separate service-level agreement, no specific uptime percentage or uninterrupted availability is promised. This statement does not exclude mandatory legal rights or remedies.

**Support hours, status page, incident notice channel, and any service commitments:** [REQUIRES ALPHA CONFIRMATION]

## 3. Maintenance and updates

Alpha may need to update software, databases, or infrastructure to maintain security, compatibility, and functionality. Where practical, Alpha should provide advance notice of planned maintenance that materially affects online features.

Urgent security or stability work may require shorter notice. Alpha should communicate material effects and restoration information when it is safe and practical to do so.

**Maintenance notice channel and operational owner:** [REQUIRES ALPHA CONFIRMATION]

## 4. Third-party services

Alpha's inspected architecture uses Supabase for account/backend functions. The inspected Launcher source also includes Microsoft account-related functionality. The exact contracts, subprocessors, service roles, and complete data flows must be documented in POL-002 before publication.

A third-party provider may change, limit, or discontinue its own service. Alpha should explain material effects on Alpha features where reasonably possible. Alpha does not control third-party availability or third-party terms.

**Confirmed provider list and service roles:** [REQUIRES ALPHA CONFIRMATION]

## 5. Service changes and discontinuation

Alpha may develop, change, replace, or discontinue features. Before a material change, Alpha should consider user impact, account data, saved progress, digital entitlements, and any applicable contractual or consumer obligations.

Do not promise that user data will always be exportable, preserved, or recoverable unless the relevant process has been verified.

When reporting an issue, provide the affected Alpha feature, approximate time, product/build version, and a description of the observed behavior. Avoid sending passwords, authentication tokens, or unrelated personal data.
